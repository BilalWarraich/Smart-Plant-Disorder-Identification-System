# Smart Plant Disorder Identification System
My project during university final years where i collabroted with a developer from srilanka who was responsible for machine learning part and i was working on blockchain and backend API plus a minimal frontend, 2019. 
A distributed platform connecting farmers, agriculture experts, and vendors, so farmers could identify plant disorders and find the right products faster.

## Why I built this
My family has farmed rice and wheat in Pakistan for generations. I grew up watching how much of that work depends on knowledge that isn't always easy to reach. This was my first attempt at closing that gap with software.

## What it does
Its basically takes pictures of plant leafs and stuff and through that i identifies the any plant disease or anything plant needs for nourishments.
It basically sends the data to farmers and then they can get expert advice later vendors can get the whole history.
## Stack
Hyperledger Fabric, Golang, Hyperledger Node SDK, HTML, CSS, Jquery.
## Status
Student project, not maintained since 2020. Kept here because it's where my interest in building for real-world problems started.
